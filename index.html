Did You Know?

Interesting linguistic facts to expand your knowledge.

Arabic is written right to left, but numbers within Arabic text are written left to right.

Classical Arabic has no capital letters — letter shapes adapt based on their position in a word.

Many English words — like "algebra," "coffee," and "cotton" — originate directly from Arabic.

The Arabic script is used for multiple world languages including Persian, Urdu, and historical Ottoman Turkish.
